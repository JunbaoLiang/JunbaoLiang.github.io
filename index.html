About

I use accurate molecular simulation to turn complex trajectories into clear, testable physical insight.

My work spans enzyme catalysis, glycoengineering, peptide hydration, and battery electrolytes. Across these systems, I build force-field parameters, automate simulation campaigns on HPC resources, and develop Python analyses that connect atomistic behavior to experimentally relevant mechanisms.

Selected work

How does Asp10Asn disrupt β-phosphoglucomutase catalysis?

Electrostatic Preorganization and Mg²⁺ Positioning

First author · Major revision
  • 12 simulations
  • ~43k atoms each
  • 3.0 μs total

Projected electric-field and GROMOS cluster analyses revealed Mg²⁺ mispositioning in the mutant and disruption of ground-state electrostatic preorganization.

  • AMOEBA
  • Electric fields
  • GROMOS clustering
  • Poltype2

How can synthetic glycosylation promote enzyme product release?

Molecular Mechanism of DszB Glycoengineering

Published · 2025
  • 27 simulations
  • >70k atoms each
  • 4.05 μs total

Serine glycosylation strengthened loop-water interactions, increased loop flexibility, promoted product release, and preserved reactant positioning.

  • Polarizable MD
  • Enzyme engineering
  • Tinker9
  • MDAnalysis

How do glucose concentration and sequence order reshape peptide hydration?

Systematic Benchmark of Peptide Hydration Dynamics

Manuscript in preparation
  • 120 systems
  • 4 concentrations
  • 12–14.4 μs total

Increasing glucose concentration slowed hydration water, while sequence order altered intramolecular hydrogen bonding, peptide conformation, and surrounding water dynamics.

  • AMOEBA
  • VACF / DACF
  • SciPy
  • Hydration dynamics

Which diglyme electrolyte formulation enables the fastest Na⁺ transport?

Molecular Dynamics of Sodium-Ion Battery Electrolytes

Second author · In preparation
  • 6 simulations
  • ~45k atoms each
  • 600 ns total

The 0.6 M NaPF₆ formulation produced the fastest Na⁺ diffusion at 1.33 × 10⁻⁶ cm²/s, consistent with spectroscopy and electrochemical measurements.

  • AMOEBA / CHARMM
  • GROMACS
  • RDF / diffusion
  • Ion transport
