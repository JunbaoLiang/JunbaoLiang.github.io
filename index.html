About

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Computational Chemistry at Virginia Tech, advised by Valerie Welborn.

My research follows the full path from physics-driven simulation to predictive model. I care about every step of that path, from the atomistic details only accurate simulation can reveal, to the models that speed up work like virtual high-throughput screening. And I evaluate each step rigorously, so the results can be used with confidence.

Recently that's spanned two fronts. In biophysics, I used XGBoost to build a binding-pocket predictor reaching F1 ≈ 0.92 and published a Protein Science study on enzyme glycosylation. In energy materials, I have a submitted manuscript on ion transport in sodium-ion battery electrolytes, and a second project where I'm parametrizing a high-accuracy force field these systems have lacked, then using molecular dynamics to predict their experimental SAXS curves. Both rest on the same foundation: accurate molecular simulation, with machine learning where it sharpens the prediction.

Selected work

Binding-pocket prediction from protein structure

2024

Pocket prediction is the first step of structure-based virtual screening: it tells docking where to look, narrowing a large compound library before wet-lab testing. Built a grid-based pocket-detection pipeline with an XGBoost classifier trained on geometric and pharmacophore features from local protein neighborhoods, reaching F1 ≈ 0.92 on held-out data. Scaled inference with cKDTree spatial indexing and exported probability-scored pockets as PDB for visualization.

Electrostatic Preorganization and Mg²⁺ Positioning in β-Phosphoglucomutase

2026 · submitted

Mapped the active-site electrostatics of wild-type β-PGM and its Asp10Asn mutant with AMOEBA polarizable MD and projected electric-field analysis. Decomposing the field by residue, ion, and solvent revealed that catalysis is governed less by Asp10 proton transfer than by Mg²⁺ positioning: in the mutant, a mispositioned Mg²⁺ opposes phosphoryl transfer, revising the accepted mechanism. First-author manuscript, submitted 2026.

  • AMOEBA MD
  • Electric fields
  • Poltype2
  • Tinker

Glycosylated enzyme to reduce product inhibition in DszB

2025 · published

Artificially glycosylated DszB with β-glucose and predicted loop-dynamics changes with AMOEBA MD. Connected glycation to reduced product trapping through RMSD/RMSF and Ramachandran analysis. Published in Protein Science.

  • AMOEBA MD
  • RMSD / RMSF
  • Tinker

Electrolyte selection for sodium-ion batteries

2026 · submitted

Sodium-ion batteries are a cheaper, more abundant alternative to lithium, and their performance hinges on the electrolyte. Simulated sodium-ion electrolytes in diglyme (NaPF₆, NaOTF) with AMOEBA and CHARMM, quantifying ion transport and solvation structure via RDF and diffusion to rationalize experimental trends and guide electrolyte choice. Manuscript submitted.

  • AMOEBA / CHARMM
  • GROMACS
  • RDF · diffusion
